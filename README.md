# My Favorite Movie: *The Conjuring*

**Title:** The Conjuring  
**Genre:** Horror / Supernatural  
**Directed by:** James Wan  
**Release Year:** 2013  

---

## Why I Like It
- It’s based on true paranormal cases, which makes it more thrilling.  
- The suspense and storytelling are really well done.  
- I enjoy how it mixes mystery and horror without too much gore.  

---

## Favorite Characters
1. **Ed Warren** — a brave and devoted paranormal investigator.  
2. **Lorraine Warren** — caring, strong, and gifted with psychic abilities.  

They work together to help families facing supernatural experiences, and their teamwork makes the story more meaningful.  

---

## Short Summary
> *The Conjuring* follows Ed and Lorraine Warren as they investigate a terrifying haunting at the Perron family farmhouse.  
> Their courage and faith are tested as they confront one of the most powerful demonic presences they’ve ever faced.
